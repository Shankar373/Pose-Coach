Your camera stays here.

Camera access starts only after you choose Open camera. Live frames and pose landmarks are processed locally in your browser. Pose Coach does not send camera footage or captured photos to an application backend.

  • The pose model and WebAssembly runtime load from public distribution hosts when the live guide starts.
  • Captured images are created in a local browser canvas and downloaded to your device.
  • You can pause the camera at any time. Camera tracks are stopped when the studio is closed or the page is unloaded.

Pose guidance is a composition aid, not a guarantee of photographic quality or a body assessment.
